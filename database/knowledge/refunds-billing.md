# Refunds and Billing

## When refunds appear

After we approve a refund, it takes 5 to 10 business days to appear on your card or bank statement. The exact timing depends on your bank, not on us. The approval date, not the original purchase date, starts the clock.

## Refund method

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card or as store credit unless the original card has been closed.

## Duplicate or double charges

If you see two charges for one order, one is almost always a temporary authorization hold that drops off within 3 to 5 business days. If both charges still post after a week, contact support with both transaction dates and we reverse the duplicate.

## Updating your payment method

To change the card on file, open Billing in your account settings and add a new payment method. Set it as the default, then remove the old card. Changing your card does not affect orders that have already shipped.

## Canceling a subscription

You can cancel a subscription any time from the Billing area. Cancellation stops future charges; it does not refund the current period, which stays active until the end of the billing cycle.
