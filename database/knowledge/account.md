# Account Management

## Resetting your password

On the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password" and enter your email. We send a reset link that stays valid for one hour. If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check spam, then request a new link.

## Changing your email address

Open Account settings, edit your email, and confirm the change from the verification message we send to the new address. Until you confirm, sign-in and notifications continue to use the old address.

## Two-factor authentication

Enable two-factor authentication under Security in your account settings. We support authenticator apps and SMS codes. Save your backup codes somewhere safe; they are the only way back in if you lose your device.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from the bottom of Account settings. Deletion is permanent and removes your order history after a 30-day grace period, during which you can still recover the account by signing in.

## Downloading your data

To get a copy of your personal data, request an export under Privacy in your account settings. We prepare a downloadable archive and email a secure link within 48 hours.
