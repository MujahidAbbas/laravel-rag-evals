# Returns Policy

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The 30 days are counted from the date the carrier marks your order as delivered, not the date you placed the order.

## Condition requirements

Items must be returned unused and in their original packaging, with all tags and accessories included. We accept items you have opened and inspected, but items that show clear signs of use may receive only a partial refund.

## How to start a return

Open your order in the account area, choose the item, and select "Return". We email you a prepaid shipping label. Drop the package at any carrier location within 14 days of requesting the return.

## Restocking fee

Most returns are free. A 15% restocking fee applies only to large items marked "bulky" on the product page, and to opened items that can no longer be resold as new.

## Refunds after a return

Once our warehouse receives and inspects your return, we issue the refund to your original payment method. Inspection takes up to 3 business days. See the billing policy for how long the refund then takes to appear on your statement.
