# Exchanges

## Exchanging for a different size or color

If you want a different size or color rather than your money back, request an exchange instead of a return. Exchanges ship free and skip the restocking fee that some returns incur.

## How an exchange differs from a return

A return sends an item back for a refund to your original payment method. An exchange swaps it for a different variant of the same product. Choose "Exchange" rather than "Return" on the order so we hold your replacement.

## Exchange window

Exchanges follow the same 30-day window as returns, counted from delivery. We ship the replacement as soon as the carrier scans your original item, so you are not left waiting for it to reach our warehouse first.

## Out-of-stock replacements

If the variant you want is out of stock, we hold the exchange for 14 days. If it does not restock in time, we convert the exchange to a refund automatically and email you.
