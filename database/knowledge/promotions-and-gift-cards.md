# Promotions and Gift Cards

## Applying a promo code

Enter promo codes at checkout in the "Discount" field before payment. Only one promo code applies per order, and a code cannot be added after the order is placed.

## Promo codes and refunds

If you return an item bought with a promo code, the refund is the amount you actually paid after the discount, not the original list price. The promo code itself is not reissued.

## Gift cards

Gift cards never expire and can be combined with a promo code. A gift card balance is applied before your card is charged. Refunds for items paid with a gift card go back to the gift card balance, not to a bank card.

## Price adjustments

If an item you bought drops in price within 7 days, contact support for a one-time price adjustment refund of the difference. Price adjustments do not apply to items bought with a promo code.
