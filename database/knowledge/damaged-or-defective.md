# Damaged or Defective Items

## Item arrived damaged

If your order arrives broken or damaged in transit, do not use the normal return flow. Report it within 48 hours with photos and we ship a replacement immediately at no cost. This is faster than a return and skips the inspection step.

## Defective on arrival versus warranty

An item that is faulty out of the box is handled as a damaged-on-arrival case, not a warranty claim. The warranty covers failures that happen later under normal use. Damage on arrival is replaced right away; a warranty claim is assessed first.

## Wrong item received

If we shipped the wrong product, keep what you received until we confirm, then we send the correct item with a prepaid label for the mistake. You are never charged twice for our error.

## Photos speed everything up

For any damaged, defective, or wrong-item report, a clear photo of the item and the packing slip lets us approve a replacement without a back-and-forth.
