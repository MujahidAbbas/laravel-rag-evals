# Privacy and Data

## What data we store

We store your account details, order history, shipping addresses, and support messages. We do not store full card numbers; payments are handled by our processor.

## Requesting a copy of your data

You can request a full export of your personal data under Privacy in account settings. We email a secure download link within 48 hours. This is separate from your order history, which you can view any time.

## Deleting your data

Deleting your account removes your personal data after a 30-day grace period. Some records, such as invoices, are kept where law requires, in an anonymized form that is no longer linked to you.

## Marketing preferences

Manage marketing emails under Communication preferences. Turning off marketing does not stop transactional emails such as order confirmations and shipping updates.
