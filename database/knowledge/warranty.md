# Warranty

## Coverage period

Every product includes a 1-year limited warranty starting on the delivery date. Some product lines marked "Pro" include a 2-year warranty, shown on the product page.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use: dead pixels, failed buttons, batteries that no longer hold a charge, and components that stop working without physical damage.

## What the warranty does not cover

Accidental damage, drops, liquid damage, normal cosmetic wear, and damage from unauthorized repairs are not covered. These may be eligible for paid repair instead.

## Filing a warranty claim

Open the order in your account, choose "Warranty claim", and describe the fault. Attach a photo or short video if the fault is visible. Our team responds within 2 business days with a repair, a replacement, or a prepaid return label.

## Repair or replacement

For covered claims we repair the item when possible, or replace it with an equivalent unit when repair is not practical. A replacement carries the remaining warranty period; it does not extend it.
