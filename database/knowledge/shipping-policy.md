# Shipping Policy

## Domestic shipping

Standard shipping within the United States takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over $50. Orders under $50 have a flat $4.95 shipping fee.

## Express shipping

Express shipping delivers in 1 to 2 business days for a flat $14.95. Order before 2pm on a business day for same-day dispatch.

## International shipping

We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and most of the European Union. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days. Duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included at checkout.

## Tracking your order

A tracking link is emailed the moment your order ships. You can also find it under the order in your account. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show movement after dispatch.

## Lost or delayed packages

If tracking has not updated for 7 days, or the carrier marks a package delivered that you did not receive, contact support. We open a claim with the carrier and ship a replacement once the claim is confirmed.
